I built an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing store sales for January–August 2018, and ran an ABC analysis on the product range. 
Whats inside: 
🔹 Key metrics: quantity sold, total products, total customers and active sales days             
🔹 Warehouse ranking and sales trend for the top 3 warehouses 
🔹 ABC analysis: Category A brings ~70% of sales, B ~25%, and C only ~5% 
70% of sales came from just a handful of products. 
The takeaway: a small group of products drives most of the volume, so stock planning and promotions should focus there first. 
Category C items take up shelf space and attention but add very little. 
The dashboard is fully interactive: you can filter by date, warehouse, product and customer, and every chart updates instantly.
Tools: Power BI[, DAX, Power Query ]
